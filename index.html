FAQs

Do you guarantee sales or followers?

I guarantee quality work, consistency and honest reporting. Results depend on your product, budget and market.

No. The ad budget goes straight from your account to Meta. My fee covers strategy, creatives and management.

How many revisions do I get?

Two revisions per post or ad creative. Extra revisions are charged separately.

How fast can we start?

Usually within 3 to 5 working days after the advance payment.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. 50% advance, the rest as agreed. You get an invoice for every payment.

Can I cancel?

Yes, with 7 days notice. Advance for work already started is non-refundable.
